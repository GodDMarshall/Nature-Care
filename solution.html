Fence systems

Engineered fence configurations.

Every terrain and threat requires a different architecture. We deploy four proven fence types, each built for specific conditions.

Horizontal 7-Wire Fence

Our most widely deployed configuration. Seven strands of high-tensile galvanized wire carry a pulsating 12V DC current — a safe but decisive psychological barrier.

  • Live Post: standard agricultural and farm boundary protection
  • Elephance: heavy-duty variant for elephant-prone corridors
  • Spiral Live Post: for uneven terrain with frequent direction changes

Tentacle — Hanging / Curtain

For large areas with frequent elephant intrusion. 15-foot poles carry two parallel steel wires with stainless wires hanging every 2 feet, offset for complete coverage.

  • Maintenance: extremely low — no ground-contact wires
  • Coverage: forest boundaries and large plantation perimeters
  • Approved by the central wildlife authority

Rope Fence

Lifelong and unbreakable. Outperforms traditional rail fencing at significantly lower cost — ideal where visual clarity and low maintenance are priorities.

  • Durability: engineered for lifelong service
  • Cost: more economical than rail fence alternatives

Specialty Configurations

Custom builds for security challenges beyond standard agricultural boundaries.

  • Wall-Top Security Fence: mounts on existing compound walls
  • Monkey / Nilgai Fence: tuned pulse for smaller wildlife
