The Solar-Powered Security Paradigm

Traditional grid-reliant security configurations fail during power interruptions and add heavy operational overhead. Nature Care's solar-powered architectural arrays deliver uninterrupted, regulated energy pulses across expansive physical perimeters—completely decoupled from local power grids.

Fence Systems

Engineered Fence Configurations

Every terrain and threat requires a different architecture. We deploy four proven fence types, each built for specific conditions.

Horizontal 7-Wire Fence

Horizontal 7-Wire Fence

Our most widely deployed configuration. Seven strands of high-tensile galvanized wire carry a pulsating 12V DC current that creates a safe but decisive psychological barrier against intrusion.

  • Live Post: Standard agricultural and farm boundary protection
  • Elephance: Heavy-duty variant for elephant-prone corridors
  • Spiral Live Post: For uneven terrain with frequent direction changes
Tentacle Hanging Solar Fence

Tentacle — Hanging / Curtain Fence

Recommended for large areas with frequent elephant intrusion. 15-foot poles carry two parallel steel wires from which stainless steel wires hang every 2 feet — offset at 1.5-foot intervals for complete coverage.

  • Maintenance: Extremely low — no ground contact wires to manage
  • Coverage: Best suited for forest boundary and large plantation perimeters
  • Approved by the centrally governed authority mandating safety of wild animals, humans, and domestic animals
Rope Fence

Rope Fence

Lifelong and unbreakable. Outperforms traditional rail fencing at a significantly lower cost. Ideal for perimeters where visual clarity and low maintenance are priorities.

  • Durability: Engineered for lifelong service with minimal upkeep
  • Cost: More economical than rail fence alternatives
Specialty Fences

Specialty Configurations

Custom configurations for unique security challenges beyond standard agricultural boundaries.

  • Wall-Top Security Fence: Mounts on existing compound walls for added deterrence
  • Monkey / Nilgai Fence: Tuned pulse for smaller wildlife deterrence on crop borders
