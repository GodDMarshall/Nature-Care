Core Applications

Core Industrial Applications

Our engineering teams custom-configure physical boundaries to meet the distinct operational mandates of four primary sectors:

Commercial & Industrial Logistics

The Mandate: Preventing high-value cargo theft, unauthorized entry, and vandalism across large-scale logistics hubs, warehouses, and manufacturing plants.

"Passive perimeter options like chain-link fences are easily breached in under 60 seconds. Our active arrays introduce an immediate, regulated physical deterrent backed by zone-specific telemetry."

Our systems seamlessly integrate with existing corporate CCTV and automated access gates, mapping physical security directly to your IT infrastructure.

Strategic Energy & Infrastructure

The Mandate: Protecting critical infrastructure, including solar power grids, electrical substations, and water treatment facilities, where downtime is not an option.

Because these remote facilities are often disconnected from urban monitoring, our completely off-grid, solar-powered energizers guarantee 24/7 boundary integrity even during total regional power grid failures.

Enterprise Agriculture & Forestry

The Mandate: Mitigating severe financial yield losses caused by wildlife encroachment on commercial plantations, tea estates, and managed forestry zones.

We configure multi-strand, high-tensile physical arrays designed to withstand heavy physical impacts. By utilizing safely regulated, bio-sensitive pulsing, we break animal migration paths away from assets without risking ecological harm or violating forestry protection acts.

Premium Residential Enclaves

The Mandate: Providing continuous, high-grade perimeter defense for gated communities, private villas, and corporate real estate developments.

Mounted as low-profile structural wall-top extensions, these setups offer immediate physical protection without turning the property into an eyesore. Integrated smart control units trigger immediate silent alarms or localized sirens the second a wire is grounded or severed.

Super Strong Insulator

Perfect Wire Tightener

In-situ tighteners for precise wire tensioning. All-alloy construction with galvanized pin. Rust-proof and lightweight.

Corner Post

Intermediate Post

25×25×3mm T-post. Dual coating: zinc oxide + hot-dip galvanized (>60 microns). Custom lengths available.

Support Post

25mm dia Class B pipe stay support for corner posts. Includes clamp and fasteners. Dual-coated galvanized finish.

Tata 12-Gauge HT Wire

Primary shock fence conductor. 2.5–2.59mm diameter, high-tensile. Zinc coating min 230–300 g/m². Breaking strength 740kg.

Output Cable
