Western Ghats Bio-Sensitive Wildlife Corridor

Sector: Enterprise Agriculture & Forestry

The Challenge: A 450-acre commercial plantation matrix suffered catastrophic crop-yield loss due to elephant and wild boar encroachment, requiring a solution that completely prioritized wildlife preservation laws.

Our Deployment: Installed a 24-kilometer high-tensile, multi-strand active solar array featuring dynamic pulse modulation. The system delivered a safe, psychologically discouraging shock that completely altered wildlife migration paths away from the property outline.

Verified Result: 94% reduction in seasonal crop-encroachment incidents within the first 60 days of system activation, with zero wildlife injuries reported.

150MW Photovoltaic Generation Hub

Sector: Strategic Energy Infrastructure

The Challenge: A remote solar power plant experienced recurring copper component theft along its vast, unmonitored 12-kilometer border.

Our Deployment: Executed a heavy-duty, multi-zone active perimeter array integrated directly into the facility's central SCADA control room. Supported by dedicated off-grid battery banks, the system maintains total autonomy even during external power disruption.

Verified Result: Achieved absolute asset isolation with instant localized zone alerts, reducing perimeter breach attempts to zero.

Automotive Logistics & Distribution Center

Sector: Commercial Logistics

The Challenge: Scaling-over theft risks on existing 3-meter masonry walls surrounding a high-value vehicle inventory storage yard.

Our Deployment: Engineered low-profile structural wall-top solar fence extenders paired with real-time tamper telemetry. If a wire is cut or grounded, localized sirens and automated cloud security notifications trigger instantly.

Verified Result: Successfully hardened the existing concrete boundary against modern vertical scale threat vectors without altering local building aesthetics.
