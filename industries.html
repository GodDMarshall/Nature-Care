Core applications

Four mandates. One line.

Commercial & Industrial Logistics

The mandate: preventing cargo theft, unauthorized entry, and vandalism across logistics hubs, warehouses, and manufacturing plants.

  • 60 seconds is all a chain-link fence buys you — our active arrays are an immediate, regulated deterrent
  • Integrates with existing CCTV and automated access gates

Strategic Energy & Infrastructure

The mandate: protecting critical infrastructure — solar farms, substations, water treatment — where downtime is not an option.

  • Fully off-grid energizers guarantee 24/7 boundary integrity during regional grid failures
  • Remote-ready for facilities beyond urban monitoring networks

Enterprise Agriculture & Forestry

The mandate: ending yield losses from wildlife encroachment on commercial plantations, tea estates, and managed forestry zones.

  • Multi-strand high-tensile arrays built to absorb heavy physical impact
  • Bio-sensitive pulsing redirects animal migration paths without ecological harm

Premium Residential Enclaves

The mandate: continuous, high-grade perimeter defense for gated communities, private villas, and corporate real estate.

  • Low-profile wall-top extensions protect without becoming an eyesore
  • Smart control units trigger alarms the instant a wire is grounded or severed
