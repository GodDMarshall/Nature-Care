Enterprise Agriculture & Forestry

Western Ghats Bio-Sensitive Wildlife Corridor

Challenge

A 450-acre plantation matrix suffered catastrophic crop-yield loss from elephant and wild boar encroachment — with wildlife preservation laws that had to come first.

Deployment

24 km of high-tensile, multi-strand active solar array with dynamic pulse modulation that redirected migration paths away from the property.

Verified result: 94% reduction in crop-encroachment incidents within 60 days — zero wildlife injuries.
Strategic Energy Infrastructure

Challenge

Deployment
